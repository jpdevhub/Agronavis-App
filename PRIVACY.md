What Agronavis collects, why, who else sees it, and how to have it deleted. We collect what the app needs to advise your fields and nothing for advertising, your field boundaries are treated as the most sensitive thing we hold, and your conversations with Sahayak never leave your phone.

## 1. What we collect

You give us: your email address, name, an optional phone number and photograph, your village, district and state, your preferred language, and details of how you farm — irrigation type, soil type and the crops you grow.

The app records as you use it: the boundaries you draw and the area calculated from them, the names and dates of crops you plant, tasks you complete, photographs you scan, posts you write, and a notification token for your device.

Your device location is read only when you tap the locate button while mapping a field. We do not track your location in the background.

> **Field boundaries are sensitive**
>
> A field boundary is the precise location of someone’s livelihood. We treat it as the most sensitive thing we hold, and it is never shared with advertisers or sold to anyone.

We do not collect your contacts, call logs, SMS, installed applications, browsing history or advertising identifiers.

## 2. Permissions this app asks for

Android asks your permission before any of these. Each is used only while you are in the part of the app that needs it, and none of them runs in the background.

- Camera — to photograph a leaf for a disease reading. The photograph is sent to our server to be identified and stored against your account so you can look back at it.
- Photos — only the picture you pick yourself, when you choose one from your gallery instead of taking it. We do not read the rest of your library.
- Location — read when you tap the locate button while mapping a field, and when the app works out which district your field sits in. Never read in the background.
- Microphone — only while you hold the microphone button to speak to Sahayak. Speech is turned into text on your phone and the recording is not kept or sent anywhere.
- Notifications — to deliver the alerts you have switched on, such as smoke from stubble fires upwind of your field.
- Device storage — to hold a scan or an upload while the app is working with it. Android grants this to the camera and gallery components Agronavis uses. We only ever touch the files you create or choose inside the app.

Refusing any of them leaves the rest of the app working. You can change your mind at any time in Android settings.

## 3. Your conversations stay on your phone

Sahayak runs a language model on your device. What you ask it, and what it answers, is processed on the handset and never sent to us or to anyone else. The farm details it reads to answer usefully are held in a copy on the phone.

Speech recognition and speech output use your device’s own services, governed by your phone’s settings rather than by us.

## 4. Why we process it, and your consent

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 we process your data to provide the service you have asked for, and on your consent for optional items such as location and notifications.

You may withdraw consent at any time in your device settings or by deleting your account, though features that depend on it will stop working.

## 5. Who else receives anything

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. Each party below receives only what it needs:

- Supabase — our database, sign-in and file storage; holds everything we store.
- Render — runs our API and processes your requests.
- NASA POWER — receives a field’s coordinates, to return rainfall and sunlight.
- OpenWeatherMap — receives a field’s coordinates, to return conditions and forecast.
- Mapbox — receives the map area you are viewing, to return satellite imagery.
- OpenStreetMap Nominatim — receives a field’s coordinates, to return a district name.
- Expo push service — receives your device token and the alert text, to deliver notifications.

The weather, map and geocoding services receive coordinates with nothing identifying you attached. Agmarknet, the Soil Health Card scheme and the fertiliser scheme receive nothing — we read their published data on a schedule.

Map and imagery credits: satellite imagery and maps © Mapbox, © OpenStreetMap contributors. Field monitoring uses Sentinel-2 imagery from the European Space Agency, through the Copernicus programme. Fire detections are from NASA FIRMS, and weather and soil moisture from NASA POWER.

## 6. How long we keep it

Your account, profile, farms, fields, scans and posts are kept while your account exists. Server logs are kept for 30 days.

## 7. Deleting your account

Open Profile and choose Delete My Account, directly below Log Out. This removes your profile, farms and fields, scans and their photographs, tasks, advisories and community posts. It is immediate and cannot be undone.

You can also request deletion without the app, including after uninstalling it, at https://agronavis.in/delete-account. No login is needed. We confirm the request belongs to the account holder, action it within 30 days and usually sooner, and write to you when it is done.

Cached public data such as district soil records and mandi prices is not personal to you and remains. Billing records are held for seven years because Indian tax law requires it, and encrypted backups are overwritten on their normal cycle within 90 days.

## 8. Your rights

- See everything we hold about you
- Correct anything that is wrong
- Delete your account and its data
- Receive your data in a machine-readable form
- Object to a particular use
- Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you are unable to

Write to contact@agronavis.in. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## 9. Children

Agronavis is for adults who farm. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will remove it.

Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect, and the date at the top of this page will change.
